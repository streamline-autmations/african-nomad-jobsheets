Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how African Nomad Pty Ltd ("African Nomad", "we", "us") handles information within the African Nomad Job Sheets application (the "App"), including its integration with QuickBooks Online.

Who we are

African Nomad Pty Ltd
Email: admin@africannomad.co.za

What the App does

The App is an internal job-sheet and quoting tool used by African Nomad staff to record jobs performed for clients, calculate fees, and generate quotes and invoices. Where a QuickBooks Online connection is authorized by a company (a "connected company"), the App can create and read accounting records (such as estimates, invoices, and customers) in that company's QuickBooks Online account on their behalf.

Information we collect

  • Business contact details of clients and job sites (name, address) entered by our staff.
  • Job and financial data: descriptions, quantities, rates, fees, and totals associated with a job or quote.
  • When a company connects the App to QuickBooks Online: OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Intuit, and the associated QuickBooks company (realm) identifier. We do not receive or store the QuickBooks login password.

How we use information

Information is used solely to operate the App: to generate job sheets, quotes, and invoices, and — where a company has connected QuickBooks Online — to create or read the corresponding accounting records in that company's QuickBooks Online account. We do not sell information and do not use it for advertising.

Where information is stored

Job and quote data is stored in a Supabase-hosted database used exclusively by this App. QuickBooks OAuth tokens are stored in the same database and are only ever accessed by the App's own server-side functions, never exposed to a web browser.

Sharing

Information is shared with Intuit only as required to operate the QuickBooks Online connection you authorize (i.e., the data you choose to send to or read from your own QuickBooks company). We do not share information with any other third party.

Disconnecting QuickBooks Online

A connected company may disconnect the App from their QuickBooks Online account at any time from within QuickBooks Online (Apps > My Apps). Disconnecting revokes the App's access token with Intuit and removes the stored connection from our database.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to admin@africannomad.co.za.
